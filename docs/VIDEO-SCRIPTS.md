# Voiceover scripts for the TokenValley episodes

Each episode is recorded silently from `?demo=story&ep=<id>`. Read the lines
over the video; the timestamps are where each beat starts on screen. Lines are
short on purpose, spoken at a normal pace they fit with room to breathe. Say
what is on screen a beat after it appears, not before.

Files: `exports/tokenvalley-<episode>-16x9.mp4` (YouTube, 1920x1080) and
`exports/tokenvalley-<episode>-9x16.mp4` (Reels and Shorts, 1080x1920, side
panel hidden).

Pick a licensed track from the platform's own music library when you post.
The show's theme is copyrighted, so do not use it.

## Cold opens (13 to 14 s each)

Use as standalone clips, or stitch the three together with one line of
narration at the start: "Things that happen in my AI office when I'm not
looking."

**Paper Jam.** The copier jams. Creed walks over, unplugs it, walks away.
Narration, optional: "IT support, in one move."

**All-Hands.** Michael calls an all-hands to an empty conference room and
waits. Narration, optional: "Attendance was optional."

**Creed.** Creed appears in the server closet, then the kitchen, then the lobby
couch, without walking. Jim looks at the camera. Narration, optional: "Nobody
knows how Creed gets anywhere."

## Posting notes

- Reels and Shorts: use the 9x16 files. The first three seconds are the
  title card plus the valley, so start talking on the title card.
- YouTube: the 16x9 files, the pilot as the main video and the episodes as a
  playlist.
- Captions: platforms auto-caption voiceover well. If you want burned-in
  captions, press L before recording to turn on the name cards, which gives
  viewers the character names without you saying them.
- Sound: the recordings are silent. Add the platform's music under your
  voice, low.
